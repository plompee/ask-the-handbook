# Paying your rent

Rent is due on the date shown in your tenancy agreement, usually the same day each month as your move-in date. Pay by standing order to the account in your agreement, quoting your property reference. We do not accept cash at the office.

If you think you will be late, contact us before the due date. Rent that is more than 14 days late may be charged interest at 3% above the Bank of England base rate, as the law allows.

# Reporting repairs

Report non-urgent repairs through the tenant portal with photos. We aim to contact you within 2 working days and complete most routine repairs within 14 days, depending on the landlord's approval and contractor availability.

Please let contractors in at the agreed time. If you miss an appointment without notice, a missed-visit charge may be passed on to you.

# Emergencies

An emergency is: no heating or hot water (October to March), a major leak, a gas smell, no electricity, a broken front door lock, or anything that makes the home unsafe.

If you smell gas, leave the property and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999 straight away. For a major leak, turn off the water at the stopcock first.

For other emergencies call our out-of-hours line on 01375 000999. Contractors aim to attend the same day. Do not arrange your own contractor unless we cannot be reached for 24 hours.

# Pets

Pets are not allowed unless your agreement says so. If you'd like to keep a pet, ask us in writing: we will ask the landlord, who will not unreasonably refuse. If agreed, the landlord may ask you to take out pet damage insurance.

# Your deposit

Your deposit is protected with the Deposit Protection Service (DPS) within 30 days of us receiving it. You will receive the DPS certificate and prescribed information by email.

At the end of the tenancy, we compare the check-out report with the inventory. We aim to agree any deductions and return your deposit within 10 working days of the check-out report being agreed. If you disagree with deductions, you can use the DPS's free dispute service.

# Moving out

Give at least one month's notice in writing, ending on a rent day, unless your agreement says otherwise. Before you leave: clean the property to the standard on the inventory, remove all belongings, return every key to the office, and send us your forwarding address and final meter readings.

Professional cleaning is only required if the property was professionally cleaned when you moved in.

# Noise and neighbours

Please keep noise down between 23:00 and 07:00. Bins must be put out only on collection day and brought back in the same day.
