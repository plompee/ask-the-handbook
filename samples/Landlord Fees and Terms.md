# Our services

Tenant-find: we market the property, carry out viewings, reference tenants, arrange the tenancy agreement and register the deposit. You manage the tenancy yourself after move-in.

Fully managed: everything in tenant-find plus rent collection, arranging repairs, two property inspections a year, gas and electrical safety reminders, and handling the check-out and deposit return.

# Fees

Tenant-find: 10% + VAT (12% inc. VAT) of the first year's rent, payable on move-in.

Fully managed: 14% + VAT (16.8% inc. VAT) of the rent collected, deducted monthly.

Inventory and check-out report: £150 + VAT per property, charged once at the start of each new tenancy.

Renewal of an existing tenancy: £90 + VAT. Serving a notice: £60 + VAT.

# Safety requirements

Landlords must provide a valid Gas Safety Certificate (renewed every 12 months), an Electrical Installation Condition Report (every 5 years), an Energy Performance Certificate rated E or above, and working smoke alarms on every floor plus carbon monoxide alarms where there is a fixed combustion appliance.

We will not let a property until these are in place.

# Repairs on managed properties

We can approve repairs up to £250 + VAT without asking you first. Above that we will get your approval, except in an emergency where the delay would cause more damage or put tenants at risk.

# Ending our agreement

Either side can end the management agreement with two months' written notice. If you end a tenant-find agreement and the same tenant stays on, the fee for the first year is still due.

# Payments to landlords

For managed properties, rent received by the 25th is paid to you by the last working day of the month, with a statement showing fees and any repair costs.
