# Working hours and attendance

The office is open Monday to Friday 09:00-17:30 and Saturday 10:00-14:00. Full-time staff work 37.5 hours a week and take part in the Saturday rota, roughly one Saturday in four. Saturday shifts are paid as a normal day and give you a day off in lieu the following week, to be agreed with your manager.

If you are going to be late, call your manager before 09:00. Text messages are not enough.

# Holidays

Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave plus bank holidays. The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, but they must be used by 31 March.

Book leave through the HR system at least four weeks in advance for anything longer than three days. No more than two people from the same team can be off at the same time, and leave in the last two weeks of December is limited because of year-end lettings renewals.

# Sickness

If you are ill, call your manager by 08:45 on the first day. For absences of up to seven calendar days you can self-certify using the form on the HR system. From the eighth day you need a fit note from your GP.

You receive full pay for up to 10 days of sickness in a rolling 12-month period, then Statutory Sick Pay. A return-to-work chat with your manager takes place after every absence.

# Remote working

Negotiators and valuers are office- and field-based. Lettings administration and accounts staff may work from home up to two days a week once they have passed probation, by agreement with their manager. When working from home you must be reachable on Teams and the office phone app between 09:00 and 17:30.

# Expenses and mileage

Business mileage in your own car is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that. Travel between home and the office is not business mileage.

Submit expenses through the HR system by the 25th of each month with photos of receipts. Claims older than three months will not be paid. Client entertainment over £50 needs approval from a director before you spend it.

# Data protection

Client and tenant data must only be stored in the CRM, never on personal devices or in personal email. Do not share a tenant's contact details with a landlord or contractor without a reason connected to the tenancy.

If you think personal data has been lost or sent to the wrong person, tell the office manager immediately - we have 72 hours to report serious breaches to the ICO. Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.

# Probation and reviews

New starters have a six-month probation period with reviews at three and six months. After probation, performance reviews take place every April and October. Pay rises are reviewed once a year in April.

# Dress code and conduct

Smart business dress is expected in the office and at appointments: no jeans or trainers. Company polo shirts may be worn on Saturdays.

Never accept gifts from clients worth more than £25 without telling your manager. Treat colleagues, clients and tenants with respect; bullying or harassment of any kind is a disciplinary matter.
